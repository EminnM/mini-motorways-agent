### Every mismatch found, and its explanation
1. **Clock snap on restore.** Right after restore the exported `simulationTime` is 30.000 where the snapshot read 29.900 (0.1 s = one frame); nothing else in the tile JSON differs (`tilesEqualExceptClock` true on 12/12 restores). After the next decision the full observation is identical to the original's (3b). Consequence: branch spawn/score times are measured on a clock ≤ 0.1 s ahead; comparisons use a 1-decision tolerance.
2. **Transient observation drift in the normal path**, independent of the op: with the **pre-patch DLL** swapped back in, 2 of 4 fresh episodes differed from the S22 log at decisions 0–7, 16 and 22 and then re-matched (`prefork_dll_drift_check.json`); with the new DLL the same pattern appeared in 1 of 6 runs plus a recurring single-decision difference at 127. These are native (likely frame-alignment / vehicle-count timing) and pre-exist the patch; whole-episode determinism was 11/12 in S22.
3. **Spawn-key granularity**: the fork logs one key per destination model (first tile) whereas raw observations list 4 destination tiles — a comparison artefact, handled in the validator by collapsing blocks.
No invariant failed after these were accounted for.

## 3. Answers
1. **Is DIAG_FORK operational?** Yes. Request/response schema in `plugin_patch/DIAG_FORK.cs`; Python driver `harness.ForkSession`, parser/metrics/confound test in `harness.py` (offline tests 4/4, live tests 5/5).
2. **What it restores.** The game's own save format (`ExportSim` / `RestoreGameFromBytes`, 65–92 KB at decisions 30–90) plus `SnapshotExtras`: city PRNG state (verified equal), behaviour-model PRNG (upgrade offers), destination `ActivationTime`, transient demand counters, lane speed/bookkeeping, inbound-lane order (restore note: "prng copied; activation n/n; lanes n/n; inboundLanes reordered; vehicles n/n"). Verified identical after restore: PRNG string, tiles (roads incl. Mothballed/Pending, content, groups, destination demand/overcrowding), vehicle counts after one step, legal mask, score/concrete/globals. Not independently verified: per-vehicle positions/speeds at the restored instant (only the per-tile counts after one decision), scheduled-building lists (only indirectly via identical spawn sequences for 60 decisions), the 2 residual divergences recorded in `research/cf_probe/RESULTS.md` (restore-of-restore; not reproduced here).
3. **Causal comparisons it enables.** From one snapshot: any set of action-list interventions vs an empty baseline (and duplicate baselines), with WAIT continuation up to a horizon — spawn placement/type/timing, PRNG trajectory (so blocking vs stream-shift can be told apart per branch), concrete cost, score at checkpoints, terminal step. Because the game is left at a restored state, Python can also continue a branch with any policy through normal STEP calls (one branch per fork call, re-forking for the next).
4. **Remaining confounders.** (a) PRNG-stream shift from any road edit (detectable, not avoidable without a candidate-aware sham); (b) the 0.1 s clock snap (bounded; use decision tolerance); (c) native transient drift (duplicate baseline per anchor catches it); (d) continuation policy: WAIT continuation isolates spawns but not deliveries under play — policy continuation needs one fork per branch and inherits planner behaviour differences; (e) only per-tile vehicle counts are observable for traffic.
5. **Smallest defensible next experiment.** Re-run the S22 throughput question on this instrument, phase 1 only: 12 seeds × anchor 60 (planner-B main line) × branches {base, base2, V1 parallel, V2 dense} with WAIT continuation for 60 decisions — equivalent starting state by construction, spawn divergence now an *outcome* (with the PRNG confound flag), primary outcome deliveries per connected-house-decision up to the first spawn difference or 60 decisions, duplicate baseline as the noise floor. ≈ 12 × 4 × ~8 s ≈ 7 min native. Only if that shows a throughput difference is a policy-continuation phase (one fork per branch, planner B continuing) worth its cost. The spawn pilot stays deferred.
