# S32 — Read-only audit: can the hybrid agent place roads outside the revealed playable area?

**Verdict: a violation is possible by construction, at the east and south edges of the revealed area.** The plugin confines the *origin* tile of every probed ADD to the native playable-area-at-time rectangle, but the probe directions are East and South, so edges whose far endpoint is one tile beyond the rectangle's east or south edge are enumerated as legal and applied without any area check. Empirically 11–12 % of all legal ADD actions are such boundary-crossing edges. Both PLAN and EDIT can take them. Whether the trained policy actually did cannot be determined from the hybrid logs (action coordinates are not recorded). Nothing was modified; the corrective change is proposed below for approval.

## 1. Native definition of the revealed/playable area (not the camera viewport)
* Full simulation grid: `CityDefinition.PlayableArea` = RectInt(−18, −10, 37 × 21) — this is the observation/action index space (777 tiles).
* Revealed area: `City.GetSimulationPlayableAreaAtTime(expansionTime, ForceWholeTiles)` (`City.cs:188–191`), a rectangle that grows with `ClockModel.ExpansionTime` through the game's unlock schedule (`GetCameraSizeAtTime` → `GetSimulationPlayableAreaAtZoom`). It is the game's own mechanic, used by the building placer (`BuildingPlacer.cs:255–263`) and by every player action: `City.IsTileInPlayableArea(coord, expansionTime)` (`City.cs:239`) is required for **both tiles of each drawn road segment** (`ControllerDrawRoadAction.cs:155`: `IsTileInPlayableArea(currentPosition) && IsTileInPlayableArea(nextTilePosition)`; mouse path `DrawRoadAction.cs:102`, road-end check `ControllerDrawRoadAction.cs:120`), for house drags, roundabouts (`DragRoundaboutAction.cs:79`) and motorway pins (`AddMotorwayAction.cs:309`). The stock game therefore never lets a player build a road whose far end is outside the revealed rectangle.

## 2. Plugin: EnumerateLegalActions and the ADD mask (`Entrypoint.cs`, "REACH RULE" block)
* Computes the same rectangle (`reachCity.GetSimulationPlayableAreaAtTime(expT, ForceWholeTiles)`), intersects it with the bounds, and loops over **origin** tiles inside it, probing `TileEditor.AddRoad(tilemap, coord, East|South)` — the real native validator — and adding `Add(coord, dir)` when the probe succeeds. The adjacent (far) tile is `coord + (1,0)` or `coord + (0,−1)`; **it is never tested against the rectangle**. At the rectangle's east column every East probe, and at its south row every South probe, has its far endpoint outside the revealed area. (North and west crossings cannot occur: such edges are the outside neighbour's South/East edge, whose origin is outside the loop.)
* `TileEditor.AddRoad` (`TileEditor.cs:40–110`) checks only static map properties — `TileIsBuildable`, water/rail/mountain passages, carparks, houses — not the expansion rectangle. So the native validator does not close the gap; in the stock game the gap is closed one layer up, in the input actions listed above.

## 3. Python side (no transformation adds actions)
`MiniMotorwaysEnv.action_masks` = `legalActionIndices` verbatim; `HybridNativeEnv` copies it and appends PLAN (legal iff WAIT is legal); S11/S12 guards only remove actions; the S18-H adapter's native-legality patch makes the planner's buildability *defer to the mask* (`native_adapter.py`), and the executor emits an action only if `wm.legal_now` (the mask) allows it. Nothing on the Python side can add an action the plugin did not enumerate; nothing removes the crossing edges either.

## 4. ApplyAction
`ApplyAction → TryAddRoad → TileEditor.AddRoad` again (`Entrypoint.cs:2377–2400`), then `EditTileCommand` is scheduled — same checks as the probe, no area check. `TryAddMotorway` (M2) likewise checks nothing beyond the native editor, so a motorway endpoint outside the revealed rectangle would also be accepted (the stock UI forbids it at `AddMotorwayAction.cs:309`).

## 5. PLAN vs EDIT
Both are subject to the identical mask: EDIT = the policy sampling a masked index; PLAN = planner B whose candidate edges are filtered by `legal_now` (mask) and whose structural buildability is the mask itself in native mode. So both can select crossing edges; neither can select anything the plugin did not enumerate. The planner's Dijkstra heads toward destinations inside the revealed area, so it rarely has a reason to use a crossing edge; S8-C-style EDITs have no such bias.

## 6. Evidence from recorded state
* Offered legality (recorded masks): at the 12 S24 anchors (decision 200) **185 of 1,523** legal ADD edges (12.1 %) have their far endpoint outside the bounding box of all legal origin tiles, i.e. beyond the revealed rectangle (e.g. seed 500000: `ADD(−15,−7,S)` … `(−12,−7,S)` reach row −8 while the lowest origin row is −7); in the 48 S7 native captures **836 of 7,484** (11.2 %).
* Accepted crossings by the hybrid agent: **not determinable** — the hybrid training/evaluation logs record action *kinds* only, and no log stores the per-decision rectangle. The planner logs (S21/S22/M1) store action indices but not the rectangle; a re-simulation would be needed to count them. Not done (read-only audit).

## 7. Classification
* Origin tile inside the revealed area: **by construction** (plugin loop).
* Far endpoint inside the revealed area: **not enforced** (violation possible by construction; frequency of offered crossing edges 11–12 %; actual use unmeasured).
* Everything else about buildability (terrain, passages, carparks, houses): enforced by the native `TileEditor.AddRoad` at both probe and apply time — by construction, and confirmed by 0 illegal actions in 100+ episodes.
Edge cases: the rectangle is recomputed every decision, so an edge that was legal may become part of a larger area later (no issue); the playable area can also shrink? No — it only grows with expansion time. Motorway endpoints (M2 interface) share the gap.

## 8. Smallest corrective change (NOT applied; for approval)
In `EnumerateLegalActions`, before probing `AddRoad`, require the far tile to be inside the same rectangle: `Vector2Int far = TileUtilities.GetAdjacentCoordinates(coord, dir); if (far.x < loopMinX || far.x > loopMaxX || far.y < loopMinY || far.y > loopMaxY) continue;` (equivalently `reachCity.IsTileInPlayableArea(far, expT)`). Defence in depth: the same two-tile check in `TryAddRoad` (reject with "outside playable area") and both endpoints in `TryAddMotorway`. Effect: the action space, masks and all observations are unchanged; only the 11–12 % boundary-crossing ADDs disappear from the legal set. Consequence for existing results: the crossing edges were offered to every policy evaluated so far (S8-C, planners, hybrid) and legal under the game's editor, so recorded outcomes are valid game states; after the fix, deterministic planner episodes may differ slightly where the planner previously routed along a boundary, which must be re-baselined before any further paired comparison.
