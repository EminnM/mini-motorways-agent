# M2 — Motorway action capability: Stage A validated; Stage B stopped at the preregistered opportunity rule

Scope kept: additive plugin interface only (no roundabouts/lights, no new action indices), planner B unchanged, preregistered rule written before any Stage B outcome, no tuning, no RL, no synthetic work. ## Stage A — native interface (mechanics inspected read-only in the decompiled sources; interface validated live)
**Mechanics used (CONFIRMED):** a player's motorway is two edits — `TileEditor.AddUnbuiltMotorway` (pin; consumes one Motorway upgrade; endpoint must be buildable, not water/mountain/rail) then `TileEditor.AddMotorway(start, startDir, end, endDir)` (length = Chebyshev ≥ 2, any two points, crosses water/buildings, endpoints need a free 8-direction ramp slot — a road in that direction or a roundabout/light forbids it; endpoints need not be road tiles; 0 concrete). The model goes Planned → Active on the next simulation step; it is two-way, one lane per direction, enterable only at the two endpoints; lane speed 3 vs 1 for roads with no turn/junction penalty; removal (`ClearTileExplicit(…, BuiltMotorways)`) Mothballs it, release waits for vehicles, the upgrade is refunded. Ramps and roads share direction slots.
**Interface added (additive):** optional `"motorway"` field on STEP (`{op:add, x1,y1,d1, x2,y2,d2}` / `{op:remove, x,y}`) applied before the ordinary action in the same decision (callers send WAIT), with `motorwayOk`/`motorwayInfo` in the response; read-only op `MOTORWAY_CHECK` (dry-run legality, nothing scheduled, no id consumed); observation export `globals.motorways` (id, number, state, start/end tiles and ramp directions) and `motorwaysAvailableOrDraft`. Discrete(3111), masks, road ADD/REMOVE and upgrade handling untouched. One integration fix: STEP is served by the visible-mode coroutine even headless, so the field is passed to that path too. Research subclass `MotorwayEnv` (production `mini_motorways_env.py` not modified).

## Stage B — fixed motorway baseline (rule M, preregistered; 12 seeds × {noMW, ruleM}, 600 decisions)
Rule M (hop-count proxy of the travel-time model: `T_mw = hops(house→a) + 2 ramps + ⌈cheb(a,b)/3⌉ + hops(b→stub)`, place the pair with the largest summed saving over same-colour houses if saving ≥ 6 and `MOTORWAY_CHECK` legal; both arms choose the Motorway package when offered). Native wall-clock 403 s per arm (parallel).
* Upgrade availability: a Motorway package is first obtainable at decision 421 (week 3), so only **3 of 12** seeds (500000, 563352, 579190 — the three that survive past 421) ever hold a motorway; the other nine end at 145–323 decisions before the first offer.
* Opportunities: **273 decisions evaluated, 0 eligible pairs** on all three seeds (best saving < 6 everywhere; no legal-ramp failures because no pair qualified). Reason: planner B's routes are short trees (house → stub typically 3–12 hops), so `2 + ⌈cheb/3⌉` ramp-and-lane cost leaves ≤ 5 hops of saving even for the longest routes; the rule's own threshold, chosen from the cost model, is never met.
* Outcomes: ruleM ≡ noMW on all 12 seeds (deliveries 615 vs 615, identical episode lengths, identical upgrade choices); one hash divergence at decision 333 on seed 500000 with identical outcomes (native transient).
**Preregistered stop rule triggered** ("fewer than 4 seeds yield an eligible placement"): no new heuristics were tried.

## Decision
Motorways are now a **validated, executable action** for the agent (placement, legality check, removal, inventory and concrete accounting, exported state), but in the tested regime they are **not a meaningful strategic action**: the package arrives after most episodes have ended, and the planner's networks are too short for a 2-ramp motorway to shorten any trip by the rule's margin. Together with S25/S26 (deliveries capped by demand and car supply, not trip time, at ≤ 1.5 houses per destination) there is no evidence that a learned or heuristic motorway policy could change deliveries or survival here. The capability is kept (it is the prerequisite for any later-game or denser-map regime where routes are long and upgrades accumulate), and the recommendation is to **stop motorway work in this regime**: a follow-up only makes sense on cities that reach the 3rd–4th week with long routes — which, by the S21 findings, requires first solving survival to decision 400+ (colour-timing game-overs), not infrastructure.
