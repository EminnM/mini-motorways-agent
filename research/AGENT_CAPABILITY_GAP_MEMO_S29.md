# S29 — Agent capability gap assessment and milestone decision (memo; no implementation)

Basis: plugin `Entrypoint.cs` (ActionKind, ApplyAction, EnumerateLegalActions, BuildPolicyObservation), encoder `mini_motorways_env.py` + obs v2, S18-H MECHANICS.md, S21 audit, S25 routing audit, native results S21–S27. Deterministic policies reproduce decision-for-decision natively (S22/S23/S27), so planner-based comparisons on the 12 seeds are nearly noise-free — this changes what counts as a cheap experiment.

**Dominant native failure (S21 evaluation):** every episode ends by overcrowding; three of twelve seeds end at ~150 decisions because two houses belong to a colour whose destination has not spawned, so the single destination overcrowds — no road action helps; the remaining episodes end at ~300 with every house connected, where deliveries are capped by demand/car supply per destination. The agent's remaining levers over survival are therefore (a) resources and infrastructure obtained through upgrade choices, (b) infrastructure actions that are currently not executable (motorway/roundabout/light), and (c) nothing else in the tested regime.

## 2. Candidate milestones (≤ 3)

**M1 — Upgrade-aware planner (export offered upgrades; choose by rule).** Capability: export the two offered `UpgradeType`s in the observation (additive plugin field, like `pendingUpgradeChoices`), and give planner B a fixed, inspectable choice rule (e.g. concrete if concrete < next route cost, else bridge if water blocks a pending route, else motorway/roundabout by availability order). Metric: survival decisions and deliveries over 600 decisions on the 12 seeds, paired against the same planner with the current blind slot-0 choice and against random choice; exact comparisons (deterministic policy). Scope: ~40 lines plugin export + ~60 lines rule + logging of the chosen type; one 12-seed evaluation (≈6 min native). Stop: offered options are not readable from `UpgradeDatabaseModel`, or the two options are identical/never vary, or no seed's survival changes under any rule (then upgrades are not a lever in this regime).

**M2 — Motorway action family.** Capability: new ActionKind for `AddMotorwayEdit` (endpoints, legality enumeration, removal), planner candidate "connect far cluster by motorway" (typed in S18-H). Metric: deliveries and survival on seeds where a far cluster exists, vs planner B. Scope: plugin action + mask (large), mechanics verification (placement rules, cost, speed), planner integration. Stop: legality cannot be enumerated reliably, or motorways are never affordable before episode end, or trip time is shown not to matter (S28 unresolved). High cost, uncertain value — depends on M1 (availability) and on the unanswered trip-time question.

**M3 — Overcrowding-aware observation and WAIT/priority rule.** Capability: pass `overcrowdingTime` and per-destination demand into the planner's scoring so it prioritises houses of the colour closest to game-over; metric: survival decisions, paired vs planner B. Scope: encoder/planner only (no plugin change). Stop: the planner already connects every connectable house within 3–4 decisions (S21: 83–86 % within 60, latency median 3), so if no seed has a choice between colours when overcrowding starts, there is nothing to prioritise — likely outcome, cheap to confirm from the S21 logs before building anything.

## 3. Decision
**Recommend M1.** It is the only lever that is (i) currently executable through the plugin (the choice action exists; only the information is missing), (ii) mechanically known to alter the resources that gate everything else (bridges, motorways, roundabouts, lights, concrete; S21 audit D), (iii) cheap and read-only to instrument, and (iv) testable with exact paired comparisons thanks to deterministic reproducibility. M2 is the natural successor if M1 shows motorways/bridges become available and S28's measurement gap is closed; M3 should be screened from existing logs first and will probably be dropped. Nothing is implemented by this memo.
